# HW7: Analysis

## Writing

* Write the Analysis section in your team overleaf project.
* Recommended length: 100 - 200 lines (including tables and figures).
* Submit the PDF version of your current draft up to the Analysis section.
* Many conferences require a standalone **Limitations** section; write yours in Discussions for now (see [Discussions](/chapters/analysis/discussions)).

## Rubric

* **Performance Analysis** (3 points)
  * Is performance broken down along dimensions relevant to the task (e.g., data subsets, input properties, output types, efficiency or cost), with comparisons to the baseline and prior work?
  * Are the findings explained (why), not just reported (what)?
  * Are the figures and tables clear, with every claim supported by evidence?
* **Ablation Study** (2 points)
  * Does each ablation isolate a single design choice and support a claim of the paper?
  * Is there analysis of where, why, and at what cost each method helps, beyond reporting scores?
* **Error Analysis** (3 points)
  * Are errors sampled and categorized systematically, with their distribution reported?
  * Are representative errors examined in detail, with their causes (model, data, or task)?
  * Is there an interpretation of what the errors imply for the approach?
* **Discussions** (2 points)
  * Are the limitations specific and backed by evidence from the analysis (e.g., technicality, robustness, applicability)?
  * Are the future directions concrete and connected to the limitations and error findings?
