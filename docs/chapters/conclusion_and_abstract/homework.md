# HW8: Conclusion & Abstract

## Writing

* Write the Conclusion section and the Abstract in your team overleaf project.
* Recommended length: 20 - 40 lines for the conclusion, and 20 - 35 lines for the abstract.
* Submit the PDF version of your final draft, including the Conclusion and the Abstract.

## Rubric

* **Summary** (2 points)
  * Does the conclusion summarize the overall contribution and the key findings of the methods?
  * Is it conclusive, distinct from the abstract and the introduction?
* **Future Work** (1 point)
  * Are the remaining challenges clearly identified?
  * Are the next steps concrete and connected to those challenges?
* **Abstract** (3 points)
  * Does it cover the task, challenges, methods, experiments, and key findings?
  * Is it standalone and compelling, without being redundant to the introduction?
  * Does it emphasize the main contribution of the paper, supported by the key results?
